Chrome 扩展隐私政策

Chrome Extension Privacy Policy

更新 / Updated: 2026-11-18 · 扩展版本 / Extension version: 1.6.7

1. 适用范围 Scope

本政策适用于“应用街 · 找到合适的工具”Chrome 扩展（包括新标签页、工具箱、壁纸和自定义鼠标功能），以及本隐私政策网页。更新日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日。

This policy covers the App Street Chrome extension, including its new-tab page, toolbox, wallpapers and custom cursors, and this policy page. Updated November 18, 2026.

2. 本机保存的信息 Data stored on your device

扩展在当前浏览器的本地存储及 IndexedDB 中保存设置、语言、收藏、扩展内最近使用记录、快捷方式、天气城市、壁纸、自定义鼠标原图、鼠标样式及点击位置，以及缩略图、备用画面和配色缓存。这些图片和设置不由本扩展上传到开发者服务器，也没有账号云端同步。扩展内应用目录搜索在本机完成。

The extension stores preferences, language, favorites, recent activity within the extension, shortcuts, weather locations, wallpapers, uploaded cursor images, cursor styles and hotspots, and preview, fallback-frame and color caches in browser-local storage and IndexedDB. The extension does not upload these files or preferences to the developer’s servers or sync them through an account. App-directory searches are processed locally.

3. Google 登录 Google sign-in

登录是可选功能。经你授权后，扩展使用 Chrome identity 接口向 Google 取得授权令牌，并从 Google 的用户资料接口读取邮箱和基本资料，用于显示账号和头像。当前界面主要显示邮箱和头像；授权范围包括基本个人资料和邮箱。授权令牌由浏览器管理，并在当前页面内存中用于向 Google 发起请求；本扩展只在本机保存登录状态标记，不把令牌、邮箱或个人资料发送给开发者服务器。加载头像时，相应图片服务会收到正常的网络请求。退出扩展账号会清除本扩展的登录状态并移除当前缓存令牌；你也可以在 Google 账号的第三方连接设置中撤销授权。

Sign-in is optional. With your consent, Chrome identity obtains an authorization token and the extension requests your email and basic profile from Google to display your account and avatar. Requested scopes cover email and basic profile; the current interface primarily displays email and avatar. Chrome manages tokens, which are used in page memory for requests to Google. The extension locally stores a signed-in flag and does not send tokens, email or profile data to the developer’s servers. Avatar loading sends ordinary requests to the image provider. Signing out clears the extension sign-in state and removes the current cached token; authorization can also be revoked through your Google Account’s third-party connections.

4. Google 数据的使用限制 Limited use of Google data

来自 Google API 的信息仅用于提供你授权的账号展示功能，遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策和 Chrome 应用商店用户数据政策中的限制使用要求。我们不出售这些信息，不将其用于广告、交给数据经纪商，或用于训练通用人工智能模型。

Information received from Google APIs is used only for the authorized account-display feature and is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy and the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including their limited-use requirements. It is not sold, used for advertising, supplied to data brokers or used to train general-purpose AI models.

5. 天气、定位和地区名称 Weather, location and place names

经你允许，定位用于匹配附近城市；你也可以手动选城市并关闭自动定位。设备定位在本地用于城市匹配，天气请求使用所选公共城市、分区中心或粗化后的位置。新加坡公开观测数据来自 api-open.data.gov.sg；当前天气分类及其他地区天气可能请求 api.open-meteo.com。地区名称翻译可能向 geocoding-api.open-meteo.com 发送所选公共地名、国家、城市中心和界面语言。上述服务可能收到 IP 地址及其他常规请求信息。定位不用于持续跟踪、广告或出售。

With permission, location is used to find a nearby city; you may instead select a city manually and disable automatic location. Device coordinates are matched locally, and weather requests use a selected public city, district center or coarse location. Singapore public observations come from api-open.data.gov.sg; current weather classification and weather elsewhere may use api.open-meteo.com. Place-name translation may send a selected public place name, country, city center and interface language to geocoding-api.open-meteo.com. These providers may receive IP addresses and ordinary request metadata. Location is not used for continuous tracking, advertising or sale.

6. 搜索、语音输入及外部网站 Search, voice input and external sites

“搜网页”在你提交时将本次搜索词交给所选的 Google、Bing 或百度；“找应用”的目录检索在扩展内完成。语音输入由你点击启动并授权麦克风，使用浏览器的语音识别服务；浏览器服务可能在线处理音频。扩展不自行保存录音或把音频发给开发者服务器。Google Lens、AI 模式、应用街官网及其他快捷方式会打开相应第三方页面，适用该服务自己的隐私政策。

Submitting a web search sends that query to your selected provider: Google, Bing or Baidu. App-directory searches stay within the extension. Voice input starts after your action and microphone authorization and uses browser speech recognition, which may process audio online. The extension does not separately save recordings or send audio to the developer’s servers. Google Lens, AI mode, the App Street website and other links open external pages governed by their own policies.

7. 网站访问、图标和浏览信息 Website access, icons and browsing information

为在普通网页中应用自定义鼠标，扩展可识别页面元素类别并注入光标样式，也会在更新后为已打开的网页恢复光标控制。它不会因此记录或上传网页正文、表单输入内容、全局浏览历史或屏幕内容。tabs 和 scripting 权限用于页面打开、光标应用及更新；广泛网站访问权限用于让鼠标样式在不同网站生效。部分图标来自打包资源，其他图标可能从目标网站或 Google favicon 等 HTTPS 图片服务取得；这些服务会收到请求中的网站来源域名、IP 地址及常规网络信息。

To apply custom cursors across websites, the extension identifies element types, inserts cursor styles and refreshes cursor controllers in open tabs after updates. It does not use this access to record or upload page text, entered form contents, global browsing history or screen contents. Tabs and scripting permissions support opening pages and applying or updating cursors. Broad website access enables cursors across sites. Icons may be bundled or fetched from target sites or HTTPS image services such as Google favicon, which may receive the requested site origin, IP address and ordinary network metadata.

8. 第三方服务与披露 Third-party services and disclosures

涉及的服务包括 Google（登录、账号头像、搜索、favicon、以及由浏览器提供的语音等服务）、Microsoft Bing、百度、Open-Meteo、新加坡 data.gov.sg 天气接口，以及你选择打开的网站和其图标服务。只有相应功能所需的请求会交给这些服务。我们不通过扩展出售个人信息，也不加入广告跟踪或将网页内容发送给分析服务器。访问本政策网页时，网站托管服务可能处理访问所需的常规请求信息。

Relevant services include Google for sign-in, avatars, search, favicon and browser-provided services such as speech; Microsoft Bing; Baidu; Open-Meteo; Singapore data.gov.sg weather APIs; and sites and icon providers you choose to access. Requests are made as needed for the corresponding features. The extension does not sell personal information, include advertising trackers or transmit webpage contents to analytics servers. Hosting services for this policy page may process ordinary request metadata needed to serve the page.

9. 保存期限、删除和控制 Retention, deletion and controls

本机数据通常保留到你删除相关壁纸、鼠标样式或记录，清除扩展存储，或卸载扩展。删除样式条目不一定立即删除仍被当前编辑配置引用的原图；清除全部扩展存储或卸载可删除扩展的本机数据。Google 授权由 Google 账号单独管理，卸载不等同于撤销账号授权。你可以关闭定位或语音权限、退出 Google 登录，并在浏览器或 Google 账号中调整对应授权。向开发者主动发送邮件时，开发者会收到该邮件及你自愿提供的内容，用于回复请求。

Local data generally remains until you delete the relevant wallpapers, styles or activity, clear extension storage or uninstall. Removing a saved style may leave source images still referenced by the current editing configuration; clearing all extension storage or uninstalling removes extension-local data. Google account authorization is managed separately; uninstalling does not itself revoke it. You may disable location or microphone permission, sign out and adjust browser or Google account grants. If you email the developer voluntarily, the developer receives your email and any information you provide in order to respond.

10. 安全、更新与联系 Security, updates and contact

网络接口使用 HTTPS。权限只用于相应功能；本地保存不等于额外提供加密保险库。功能或数据处理方式变化时会更新本政策。开发者联系方式：getyingyongjie@gmail.com。

Network APIs use HTTPS. Permissions support their respective features; local storage is not an additional encrypted vault. This policy will be updated when features or data handling change. Developer contact: getyingyongjie@gmail.com.
